Privacy Policy

Il-Kelma tal-Aħħar · Android game
Effective date: 19 November 2026

Rixa Studios develops Il-Kelma tal-Aħħar, an educational Maltese-language word game. We respect players' privacy and aim to keep the game simple and privacy-conscious.

Information used by the game

The game does not require an account. Game preferences and progress may be stored on your device so the game can remember them between sessions. This information is not intentionally transmitted to Rixa Studios by the game.

Optional feedback

If you choose to send feedback, the game opens your device's email application with a message addressed to rixastudios@gmail.com. No email is sent automatically. If you decide to send it, we receive the email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use that information to read and respond to feedback, address problems and improve the game. Email delivery and storage are also subject to your email provider's practices.

Advertising and analytics

In the current version, Rixa Studios has not intentionally integrated advertising networks, in-app purchases, Firebase Analytics or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We will update this policy if these practices change.

Children and families

The game is intended to be suitable for a broad audience, including people learning Maltese. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sending feedback by email and should not include personal information in their messages. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the game. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us personal information, they may contact us to request its deletion.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved game data generally remains on the device until it is cleared or the app is removed, subject to the device's backup and restore settings. Feedback emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, investigate issues and maintain relevant correspondence. You may request deletion of feedback you have sent by emailing us at the address below; we may need enough information to locate the message.

Device services and third parties

The app may use standard Android services, including device backup where enabled. Google Play and the Android operating system may process information independently under their own privacy terms. Opening the email application is optional and involves the email service you choose.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect feedback received by email, but no electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact rixastudios@gmail.com.
